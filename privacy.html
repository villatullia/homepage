Privacy notice

Version dated 15 August 2026

Villa Tullia uses the information you submit to answer your enquiry and, if you choose to book, to prepare and administer the rental agreement, signatures, payment, and stay.

Information we use

This may include your name, contact details, requested dates, number and names of guests, billing and address details, messages, agreement records, payment status, and technical security records.

Why we use it

We use it to respond before a booking, perform the rental agreement, meet legal obligations, prevent misuse, and protect legitimate business and security interests.

Anonymous website analytics

We measure visits and the steps people take toward an enquiry using anonymous visitor and session identifiers. Analytics may include pages and booking steps viewed, broad device and screen categories, browser, operating system, language, timezone, referral and campaign information, and country. Country is derived from the network address using a country lookup service; the raw network address is not retained in the analytics database. Names, contact details, enquiry messages, passwords, and form contents are not included in analytics events.

Who receives it

Information is shared only where needed with the owner and the services that operate this website, deliver email, collect signatures, or process payment. Card information is handled by the payment processor and is not stored by Villa Tullia.

Retention and your rights

Unsuccessful enquiries are removed when no longer needed. Booking, agreement, and payment records are retained for the periods required by applicable Italian law. You may ask to access, correct, restrict, or erase information where the law permits, or object to certain processing.

Contact

Use the contact details shown on the Villa Tullia website for privacy questions or requests. This notice must be updated with the owner's full legal identity and contact details before the system goes live.
