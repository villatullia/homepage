Planning your stay

A villa holiday in Padenghe sul Garda.

Stay on Lake Garda's peaceful southwestern shore, within easy reach of lakeside towns, local markets, vineyards and restaurants.

How many guests can stay?

Villa Tullia welcomes up to 8 guests across four bedrooms, with generous shared spaces for families and friends.

Is there a swimming pool?

Yes. Guests can enjoy the swimming pool shared with the residence, together with Villa Tullia's private garden.

When are stays available?

The main season runs from May to October, with clear Saturday-to-Saturday weekly stays shown on the live availability page.

Why book directly?

Direct enquiries come straight to Villa Tullia, with personal availability confirmation and a clear booking agreement.
